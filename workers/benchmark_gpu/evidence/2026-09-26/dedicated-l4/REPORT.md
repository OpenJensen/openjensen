## Setup, validation and budget

Six native regression tests passed with zero skips after version alignment. Both custom engines passed complete-action admission. Native dependencies pass their package check. TensorRT-LLM uses the documented `datasets==4.8.5` and `numpy==2.5.3` overrides for its otherwise conflicting declared constraints; its package check reports exactly those two incompatibilities. This validates the custom inference adapter, not unrelated TensorRT-LLM features.

The CUDA-only VM image initially lacked NVIDIA EGL. The first quality attempt failed before producing a score. Installing the matching `libnvidia-gl-580-server` package added graphics support without replacing the compute driver; a preflight confirmed actual NVIDIA L4 rendering. Failed setup and pre-alignment timing attempts remain in the local raw archive and are excluded from these tables.

The user authorized a $6 budget. The Console estimated compute plus the 200 GiB disk at about $0.88/hour; a four-hour automatic VM deletion deadline and auto-delete boot disk were set before execution. After verifying the final downloaded archive, the VM was explicitly deleted. Both the VM and disk were confirmed absent at 16:39:47 UTC, about 96 minutes after creation. Estimated compute plus disk cost was **$1.40**. Networking, external IP and tax are outside that estimate; this is not a finalized bill.
